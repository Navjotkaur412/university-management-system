About the Portal

Our Student & Course Portal gives students, faculty, and administrative staff a single place to manage academic records. From here, students can be enrolled and tracked throughout their program, while course structures — including semesters and subjects — stay organized and easy to update as curricula evolve. Built for speed and simplicity, the portal keeps everything in sync in real time, so changes made to a student's record or a course's semester plan are reflected instantly across the system. Whether you're registering a new student or updating next semester's subject list, everything you need is just a few clicks away.
